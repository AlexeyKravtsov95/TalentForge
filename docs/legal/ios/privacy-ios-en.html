Privacy Policy — TalentForge for iPhone and iPad

Updated: {{PUBLICATION_DATE}}

About this policy

This policy describes data handling in the TalentForge app for iPhone and iPad (iOS and iPadOS) and on the TalentForge Legal information website. The Android app has its own policy. App and site operator: Alexey Kravtsov. Contact: alexey.kravtsov95@gmail.com.

1. Data in the app

TalentForge is a talent-build planner. The app works without an account or registration, shows no advertising, and contains no built-in analytics or tracking.

The app stores builds you create on your device: character class, talent allocation, game-data version, an optional name you enter, and creation, modification, and last-opened timestamps. This information stays in the app’s private storage on your device. The app does not send builds, their names, or their talent allocations to the update server or to the developer.

2. Talent data updates

This section applies to app versions that download talent data updates: they have a “Check for updates” button on the About screen. Versions without this button make no network requests.

To keep talent data current, the app downloads a public list of updates and game-data files from the ota.mythweld.com server over a secure HTTPS connection. The app does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, files, or notifications.

The app contacts the server:

  • after it starts;
  • from time to time when you return to the app;
  • when you tap “Check for updates”;
  • when you open a saved or imported build whose game-version data is not on the device yet.

The app performs automatic update checks by itself. There is currently no separate switch in the app to turn them off. You can turn off cellular data for the app in iOS Settings or disconnect the device from the network.

The set of requested files may differ between users. For example, when you open a build for a different game version, the app downloads the files for that version. The requested files can indicate which game-data version the app needs. The builds themselves, their names, and their talent allocations are not transmitted.

With each HTTPS request, the delivery infrastructure (the server and the content delivery network, CDN) technically receives your device’s IP address, the time of the request, the requested address (URL), and the User-Agent header with the app name and version, as well as technical parameters of the secure connection. The provider of this infrastructure may process technical information about requests to provide the service and keep it secure, under its own policies.

This technical information may be kept in the delivery infrastructure’s request logs after the request has been served. This policy does not state a specific retention period, because it is determined by the delivery infrastructure. The developer does not use request logs to identify you, for analytics, advertising, or tracking, and does not combine them with other data; if the developer has access to such logs, they are used only to deliver updates and keep the service working and secure. In the app’s App Store privacy details, these logs are listed under Diagnostics (Other Diagnostic Data) as data linked to you, because an IP address may be personal data; they are not used for tracking.

The app does not send advertising identifiers, device or account identifiers, cookies, build contents, or clipboard contents to the update server.

Downloaded data is verified on the device against the developer’s cryptographic signature and stored in the app’s private storage. If verification fails, the downloaded data is not used and the app keeps working with its previous data. Without an internet connection, the app keeps working with the data already available on the device.

3. Copying, importing and sharing

When you tap “Copy code”, the app places a build code on the clipboard. When you tap “Paste” on the import screen, the app reads clipboard text for local processing; iOS may ask you to allow pasting.

When you choose “Share”, the app passes the build code to the iOS share sheet. You choose the receiving app. Any further processing is governed by that app’s terms and privacy policy. The code does not contain a build name you entered.

4. Backups and retention

If your device backup (iCloud Backup or a backup to a computer) is turned on, it can include the app’s data, including your builds. Such a backup is kept under its provider’s terms; the developer has no access to it. Downloaded talent data updates are kept in a part of the app’s storage that is excluded from backups.

Build data stays locally until you delete it. You can delete individual builds in the app, or delete the app to erase all of its data on the device, including downloaded talent data updates. The developer does not have remote copies of your builds and cannot delete data directly from your device.

5. TalentForge Legal website

When you visit this website, your browser connects to VK Cloud hosting infrastructure. To deliver pages, the hosting provider may process connection information, including your IP address and technical request details, under its own policies.

This website does not use advertising trackers or web analytics. It saves the language preference talentforge-language in your browser’s local storage (localStorage); you can remove it using your browser settings. On your first visit, the site detects your browser language.

6. App Store and external services

Apple may independently process information about downloads, purchases, and App Store interactions under its own policies. This is separate from data transmission by the TalentForge app. If you email us, the email address and message you provide are used to respond to your request.

7. Children

The app is not designed specifically for children. The app has no accounts and does not ask users for their age or identity.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when app features or services change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated date.

For privacy questions, contact alexey.kravtsov95@gmail.com.
