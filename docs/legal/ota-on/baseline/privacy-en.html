Privacy Policy

Updated: 19 October 2026

About this policy

This policy describes data handling in the TalentForge Android app and on the TalentForge Legal information website. App and site operator: Alexey Kravtsov. Contact: alexey.kravtsov95@gmail.com.

1. Data in the app

TalentForge is an offline talent-build planner. The current production Android release works without an account, advertising, analytics, or transmission of data to the developer. It does not request Android internet access permission.

The app stores builds you create on your device: character class, talent allocation, game-data version, an optional name you enter, and creation, modification, and last-opened timestamps. This information stays in app-private storage on your device. The developer does not automatically receive it.

2. Copying, importing and sharing

When you tap “Copy code”, the app places a build code on the clipboard. When you tap “Paste” on the import screen, the app reads clipboard text for local processing.

When you choose “Share”, the app passes the build code to the Android system share sheet. You choose the receiving app. Any further processing is governed by that app’s terms and privacy policy. The code does not contain a build name you entered.

3. Backups and retention

Standard Android backup of app data is disabled. Certain device-manufacturer migration tools may work differently and are not controlled by TalentForge.

Build data stays locally until you delete it. You can delete individual builds in the app, or erase all app data through Android settings or uninstalling the app. The developer does not have remote copies of your builds and cannot delete data directly from your device.

4. TalentForge Legal website

When you visit this website, your browser connects to VK Cloud hosting infrastructure. To deliver pages, the hosting provider may process connection information, including your IP address and technical request details, under its own policies.

This website does not use advertising trackers or web analytics. It saves the language preference talentforge-language in your browser’s local storage (localStorage); you can remove it using your browser settings. On your first visit, the site detects your browser language.

5. App stores and external services

Google Play, RuStore, AppGallery, and other stores may independently process installation and store-interaction information under their own policies. This is separate from data transmission by the TalentForge app. If you email us, the email address and message you provide are used to respond to your request.

6. Children

The app is not designed specifically for children. The current Android version has no accounts and no developer collection of children’s personal information.

7. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when app features or services change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated date.

For privacy questions, contact alexey.kravtsov95@gmail.com.
